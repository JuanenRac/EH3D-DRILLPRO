Example drill files of DRILLPRO (Excellon style, in mil: 1 mil = 0.001 inch)

Load them with the Load button. They are listed from the simplest to the largest.
Start with the first ones, in the simulator (the default), before sending any job to a machine.

01_one_hole.ncd                   1 holes, 1 tool(s): one hole: the smallest job, to see that everything moves
02_three_holes.ncd                3 holes, 1 tool(s): three holes with one tool
03_two_tools.ncd                  6 holes, 2 tool(s): two tools: the program asks for the change (or the changer does it)
04_grid_10x10.ncd               100 holes, 1 tool(s): 100 holes on a 0.1 inch grid
05_small_board.ncd               58 holes, 3 tool(s): a small board: pin headers, mounting holes and vias with three tools
06_four_tools_80_holes.ncd       80 holes, 4 tool(s): four tools of 20 holes each: many tool changes
07_dense_1000_holes.ncd        1000 holes, 2 tool(s): 1000 holes scattered over the whole table with two tools
08_large_10000_holes.ncd      10000 holes, 3 tool(s): 10000 holes with three tools: the sorting and the job planning with a big file
09_huge_20000_holes.ncd       20000 holes, 4 tool(s): 20000 holes with four tools: the largest example
